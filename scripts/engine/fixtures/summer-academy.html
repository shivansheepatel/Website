Summer Engineering Academy

A four-week residential program at Example Polytechnic for students entering grades 11 and 12. Participants work in teams on a design challenge, spend two afternoons a week in the machine shop, and present a prototype in the final week.

Key dates

Applications open
Applications open January 12, 2027.
Application deadline
Application deadline: March 1, 2027 at 11:59 p.m. ET.
Program runs
The academy runs July 5 – 30, 2027.

Cost

Tuition is $1,850 CAD including residence and meals. Need-based bursaries cover up to full tuition; the bursary form is part of the main application.

Eligibility

Open to students entering grade 11 or grade 12 in September 2027 who are residents of Ontario. No prior engineering coursework is required.

What you need to apply

  • Most recent report card
  • One reference letter from a teacher
  • A short written response (see below)

Written response

Answer in 300 words or fewer:

  • Describe a problem in your community that you think engineering could help with.
  • Tell us about a time something you built did not work.
