## How dates were handled

Your spreadsheet stored dates as free text — `TBA`, `Until full`, `Posting dependent`,
`2026 deadline was Jan 11`. The site needs either a real date or nothing, so:

- Where the sheet recorded a **concrete date from a previous cycle**, it was carried
  forward one year and marked **estimated**. The card explains this, and the
  description says which prior-year date it came from.
- Where the sheet said **TBA, rolling, until full, or posting dependent**, the deadline
  is `null` and the entry is marked **not posted yet**. These are honest: things like
  City of Kitchener job postings genuinely have no single closing date.
